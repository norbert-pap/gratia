CAC payback

CAC = sales & marketing spend in a cohort's first month ÷ new customers that month. Payback = months until cumulative gross profit per customer covers CAC. "Normalized" excludes the one-off Brand Strategy line. CAC here is illustrative: most acquisition is founder and referral led, so paid CAC overstates true cost.

Talking points

Internal. Press í (or type "prep") to toggle, Esc to close.

Story

Services business with SaaS-like retention economics: revenue compounding on a concentrating, high-value enterprise base, near-zero paid acquisition, expanding gross margin.

Lead with

  • Revenue climbing 2024 → 2025 → 2026 run-rate; same-customer revenue shows expansion within accounts.
  • Revenue per customer rising: bigger engagements, deeper penetration.
  • Near-zero paid CAC; growth is founder, referral and network led.
  • Blended gross margin ~40–55%, trending up.

Corrections to own up front

  • Revenue scope: first extract was hourly-only; corrected file is all streams (revenue is higher).
  • Window: starts Jan 2024 (current entity). Pre-2024 was a prior consulting entity.
  • Basis: accrual (revenue when earned); cash extract available for payback timing. Reconciles to P&L.

Likely questions

  • Concentration: anchor enterprise logos; expanding multi-deployment; pipeline beyond the top account.
  • CAC looks high: S&M is one-time and overhead, not acquisition spend; normalized CAC is far lower; most growth is organic.
  • Cash vs accrual / DSO: recent-year cash trails accrual by collection timing. Enterprise net-60/90 terms, normal.
  • April P&L: still reconciling; still expect a profit.
