Object model · core records

ObjectPurposeKey fields
AccountCustomer or prospect company. Single record per legal entity. Hierarchies via parent-account.name, parent, segment, industry, employees, owner_team, health_score, churn_risk, NRR_value
ContactPerson at an account. Multi-account membership via junction object. Roles tracked.name, email, role, persona, department, status, opt-in, last_activity
LeadInbound or outbound prospect not yet qualified. Auto-converts on threshold or manual.name, email, company, source, score, mql_status, owner, decision_maker, status
OpportunityOpen deal. Stage-driven, with auto-roll-up to forecast. Line items linked to products.name, account, amount, stage, probability, close_date, owner, type, products[]
ProductSKU master. Price book governance. SKU-level reporting and bundling rules.name, sku, family, list_price, billing_terms, available_terms, replacement_for
SubscriptionActive customer subscription. Linked to opportunity that closed it. Renewal automation.account, opportunity, products[], start, end, mrr, status, autorenew
CaseCustomer support ticket. Linked to account; affects health score.account, contact, subject, priority, status, owner, sla_breached, resolution_time

Operating principles

1

Account as the spine

Every record (contacts, opps, cases, subs) hangs off an account. No floating leads or orphan opps.

2

Process not freeform

Stage gates prevent drift. Required fields by stage. Validation rules block bad data at source.

3

Closed-loop attribution

Every closed-won opportunity ties back to original lead source. Marketing-attributed revenue is reportable, defensible, and auditable.

4

One source of truth per fact

Stripe owns billing facts. Marketo owns lead activity. Salesforce owns relationships. Don't duplicate; pipe.

5

Self-serve reporting

Tableau CRM + Looker stand up so revenue ops doesn't become a ticket queue. Dashboards live alongside records.

6

Hygiene over coverage

Better to have 90% of records clean than 100% of records dirty. Strict required fields; permissive optionals.
