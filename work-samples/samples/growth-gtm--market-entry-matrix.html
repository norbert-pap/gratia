Recommended sequencing

Year 1 · Q1–Q2
Brazil — full entry. Local entity in São Paulo, BCB licensing, 4-person team. Pilot with 2 partner banks. Target $8M ARR by month 12.
Year 1 · Q3
Mexico — pilot mode. CNBV sandbox program, 2-person remote team, 1 partnership. Validate before committing local entity in Year 2.
Year 2 · Q1
Colombia — partner-led. No local entity; partner with regional bank for distribution. Light-touch, 1 BD lead.
Year 2 · Q3
Mexico — scale. If pilot validated, set up local entity, 4–6 person team, replicate Brazil playbook.
Year 3+
Re-evaluate Argentina + Chile + Peru. Argentina's FX situation may stabilize. Chile is small but high-quality. Peru watching for regulatory updates.

Why Brazil first

Pix is the unlock. Real-time payment infrastructure makes our embedded model feasible without prohibitive interchange. Saves 6–9 months of bank-by-bank integration.
BCB Sandbox. Regulatory clarity beats Mexico's. 6-month sandbox → 12-month conditional license → full license.
Talent depth. 4 hub cities (SP, RJ, Recife, Floripa) with ample fintech engineering talent at competitive rates.

Why we're deferring others

Argentina · FX risk. Capital controls + parallel rates make USD-revenue collection unsafe. Re-evaluate when official rate stabilizes.
Chile · scale. High-quality market but $0.4B TAM is small relative to entry cost. Better as Year 3 add.
Peru · regulatory delays. Fintech licensing framework still evolving. Watching closely; expect to revisit FY27.
