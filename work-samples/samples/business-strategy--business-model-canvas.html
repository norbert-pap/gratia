Business Model Canvas · Marketplace v3
Marketplace business model · v3 (post pivot) Take rate validated at 12%

Two-sided marketplace connecting independent creative studios with mid-market brands. Differentiator: vetted supply + fixed pricing tiers.

Iterated to v3 after the open-marketplace v1 (race-to-bottom) and the agency-roster v2 (didn't scale). v3 wins by curating supply + bundling pricing — buyers compare on quality not price.

Key Partners

  • Vetted studio network (320 active)
  • Brand agencies for white-label spillover
  • Stripe (payments)
  • Loom (handoff workflow)
  • 2 staffing partners
340 partners · NPS 68

Key Activities

  • Studio vetting + onboarding
  • Match-making (brief → studio)
  • Project orchestration platform
  • QA + dispute handling
  • Brand acquisition
Match velocity: 3.2 days median

Value Proposition

  • For brands: A vetted creative studio in 72 hrs, fixed pricing, NPS-tracked work, money-back guarantee
  • For studios: Pre-qualified leads, no business-development overhead, paid in 30 days
Both sides win on time-to-value

Customer Relationships

  • Self-serve project setup
  • Dedicated CSM for >$50K accounts
  • Quarterly creative reviews
  • Studio account manager (1:8)
CSM avg book: $1.2M

Key Resources

  • Curated studio network + ratings
  • Match-making algorithm
  • Project management platform
  • Brand customer relationships (84)
  • Engineering team (12)
Network density = moat

Channels

  • Self-serve web (60% of new logos)
  • Inbound demo + sales (35%)
  • Agency partnerships (5%)
  • Studio referrals (closed-loop)
CAC: $4,200 blended

Customer Segments

  • Mid-market brands (50–500 employees) — design needs without in-house team
  • Series A–B startups — quality + speed, no full-time hire yet
  • PE portfolio cos. — playbook-driven brand work across portfolio
84 active · avg ACV $58K

Cost Structure

  • Studio payouts (88% of GMV) — pass-through, not margin
  • Engineering + product (~$2.4M / yr)
  • Sales + marketing (~$1.8M / yr)
  • CSMs + account managers (~$1.1M / yr)
  • Platform infra (~$280K / yr)
Primarily fixed cost · scale lever = match volume

Revenue Streams

  • Take rate — 12% on every project
  • Subscription — Brand Pro tier: $1,500/mo for unlimited match + dedicated CSM (28% of brands)
  • Add-ons — Rush delivery, brand asset hosting ($240K / yr)
FY26 GMV $48M · revenue $5.8M
v3 vs v1 metrics: Take rate up from 4% → 12% (curation + bundling). NPS up from 11 → 68. Studio churn down from 38% to 9%.
