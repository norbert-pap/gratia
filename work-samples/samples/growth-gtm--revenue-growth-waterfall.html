Insight

Mid-market expansion is the engine. $4.2M from 38 mid-market customers — average $111K expansion per account. Tied to second-renewal upsell motion launched Q2.
SMB churn is the leak. 9.4% gross logo churn cost $1.8M. Three down-sells preceded by support-ticket spikes — instrumentation in flight for FY26.

Recommendation for FY26

Bet on mid-market. Add 4 enterprise AEs and 2 ABM ops; lift MM expansion target from $4.2M → $6.5M.
Stem SMB churn. Health-score model + 60-day check-in for accounts under $25K ACV. Target 9.4% → 7.0% gross churn.
