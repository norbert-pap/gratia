02 · diagnosis
Bet 1 · Portfolio focus

Cut the long tail · invest in the top 20 SKUs.

Discontinue 36 SKUs (40% of catalog, 7% of revenue). Reinvest freed capacity into volume + variant expansion of top 20. Pricing power on remaining product holds. EBITDA lift modeled at +180 bps over 18 months.

What changes: 87 → 51 active SKUs. Customer-mix simulation: 96% of revenue retained.
How we win: 6-month sunset comms, alternative-product mapping, exclusive run-out for affected accounts.

04 · bet 2
Bet 3 · Pricing courage

Take 4% on the top 12 SKUs · we've been giving away the value.

Net-pricing analysis on top 12 reveals 280 bps of leakage from over-rotation on volume rebates. Recovering 200 bps + 4% list-price hike = +$11M revenue, mostly margin. Customer churn modeled at 1.4% (within retention plan).

Sequencing: Mid-FY26 list update on 8 of 12 SKUs · paired with feature-pack release that justifies the re-rate.
Risk mitigation: Tiered customer comms (top 50 accounts call-led; rest in writing) · price-hold offer for 12 months for top 10.

07 · roadmap
Risks & mitigations

Three risks, all manageable.

Customer reaction to portfolio cuts.
Mitigation: 6-month comms · alternative-product map · top-account exclusive run-out.
Capex execution risk.
Mitigation: Dual vendor on vision QC · phased deploy · plant-by-plant validation.
Pricing pushback from sales.
Mitigation: Net-price analysis to internal stakeholders · feature pack to justify re-rate · tiered exec comms.
The bigger risk is doing nothing. Margin trend extrapolated lands FY27 EBITDA at $18M. The status quo is harder than the strategy.
