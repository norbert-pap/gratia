Outbound Sales Playbook · v2
Outbound playbook · v2 · Q1 2026 Validated · 28% reply rate

Outbound playbook for SOC buyers in the 500–5,000 employee range. Three plays, one stage model, validated objection-handling.

v2 incorporates win-loss feedback from Q4. Outbound reply rate up from 14% → 28%. Demo-to-opp conversion up 6 pp. The change: leading with a measurable claim, not a feature.

Stage model · 5 stages, gated

01 · Discover

Identify a problem

SDR-led. Triggered by ICP fit + intent signal. Goal: book a discovery call with the security buyer.

EXIT GATE → Discovery booked
02 · Qualify

Confirm pain & budget

AE-led. 30-min discovery. Confirm BANT lite — pain, authority, ballpark budget. Map ecosystem.

EXIT GATE → BANT lite confirmed
03 · Demo

Show measurable claim

Lead SE. 60-min focused demo, not a tour. Customize 2 examples to their stack. Show one number.

EXIT GATE → Tech eval scoped
04 · Tech eval

Run the gauntlet

SE-led, 14-day evaluation. Pre-scoped tests in their environment. Daily slack updates.

EXIT GATE → Tech green-light
05 · Close

Buy & sign

AE-led. Pricing presented after tech eval. Procurement runway 21 days standard.

EXIT GATE → Order form signed

Three plays

Play 1
"You just had a tabletop"
Trigger: tabletop / RTO / pen test mention · Reply rate: 32%

Trigger signal

  • Hiring SOC analyst (LinkedIn signal)
  • Compliance event mentioned (SOX, SOC 2, CMMC)
  • Recent breach disclosure or near-miss in industry
  • Security tooling consolidation (G2 reviews on competitor tools)

Why it works

  • Tabletop exercises surface tooling gaps
  • Buyer is already in a "fix this" frame of mind
  • 30-day urgency window before the next quarter's SOC review

Email script

Subject:
3 SOCs we benchmarked spent 14% less last quarter

Hi [name],

Saw [trigger event]. We benchmarked 3 SOCs your size after their last tabletop — they all consolidated 2-3 detection tools and dropped MTTR by 32%. Curious if your tabletop showed similar tool-sprawl.

15 min next week to compare notes?

– [SDR]
Play 2
"You're paying twice for detection"
Trigger: 2+ overlapping tools · Reply rate: 26%

Trigger signal

  • Two overlapping detection tools in stack (e.g., CrowdStrike + SentinelOne)
  • Recent vendor renewal announced
  • CISO hire (consolidation often follows)
  • Earnings call mentions of "operational efficiency"

Why it works

  • Tool sprawl is a known issue at this stage
  • Quantifiable savings (often 18–30% on tool spend)
  • Renewal cycles create natural buying windows

Email script

Subject:
Tools your SOC has · 12% savings benchmark

Hi [name],

Noticed [Company] runs both [Tool A] and [Tool B]. We see this pattern often after acquisitions or M&A — both stay because nobody's measured overlap.

3 of our customers in [your industry] consolidated and saved an average 18% on tool spend without coverage gap. I have a 15-min benchmark walk-through if useful.

– [SDR]
Play 3
"Compliance forced your hand"
Trigger: regulatory or audit event · Reply rate: 24%

Trigger signal

  • SOC 2 Type II in flight
  • FedRAMP authorization preparation
  • Customer contract requiring specific certifications
  • Audit finding from prior cycle

Why it works

  • Auditor mandate creates urgency + budget
  • Specific compliance gaps map to specific tooling
  • Risk of non-compliance is quantifiable in lost revenue

Email script

Subject:
SOC 2 Type II evidence · 80% reduction

Hi [name],

SOC 2 prep tends to surface tooling gaps faster than any tabletop. We've helped [3 logos in their industry] cut audit-evidence collection from 14 weeks to 3, with built-in mappings to SOC 2 controls.

Your auditor will love it. 15 min next week?

– [SDR]

Common objections + responses

"We're happy with [incumbent tool]."
"Most of our customers were too — until their first tabletop or M&A. Worth 15 min to share what they wished they'd benchmarked earlier?"
"Send me a brochure / something to read."
"Happy to. I'll also send our 1-page benchmark on [their industry]. If you skim it and want to dig in, 15 min on Friday?"
"I don't have budget."
"That's the most common version of this conversation. 14 of our last 20 customers landed without an existing line item — they recovered ~22% from consolidation. Worth modeling?"
"This isn't a priority right now."
"Fair. When does it become one? Often it's compliance cycle, M&A, or a tabletop. If [trigger] hits, mind if I check back?"
"Just send pricing."
"Will do — but pricing without context tends to mislead. 10 min to learn your environment so the number is meaningful?"

Operating cadence

Daily. SDR sets 8–10 calls + 14–18 emails. Use trigger signals from priority feed first.
Weekly. Pipeline review with manager · 2 wins / 2 losses dissected. Update playbook scripts based on objections heard.
Monthly. Metrics review: reply rates, demo conversion, tech-eval pass rate. Retire scripts under 12% reply.
