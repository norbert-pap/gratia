01 · cover
Problem

Chronic-care patients are managed in spreadsheets and 5-minute visits.

9 in 10 chronic-care visits in the US end without a structured follow-up plan. Care managers juggle 4 systems (EHR, scheduling, telehealth, billing), each holding part of the patient picture. The cost: 22% no-show rate, 38% medication non-adherence, and rising readmissions for the conditions clinics are paid to manage.

02 · problem
Solution

One platform that schedules, runs, and bills the chronic-care visit — with outcomes baked in.

What we do

  • Tele-visit + structured intake (8 chronic conditions)
  • Auto-generated care plan + patient app
  • Automated CMS Chronic Care Management billing (CPT 99490)
  • Outcome dashboards by condition + provider

Why it works

Built around the CMS Chronic Care Management billing code — every minute logged becomes a billable, defensible visit. Clinics earn $42–62 per patient per month while improving outcomes. Average clinic recoups Canopy in 4 months.

Value loop: Visits → outcomes → CMS billing → margin → more visits.
