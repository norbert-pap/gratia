Triangulation & sensitivities

Top-down anchor: Bain x Stripe 2024 sized embedded fintech at $50B by 2030 across all SMB verticals. Vertical SaaS captures ~48% per Coatue 2025 analysis = $24B TAM.
Bottoms-up cross-check: Our 38K customer plan implies 5.1% of total SAM-eligible verticals (calculated independently from FY30 hiring + win-rate model).
Take rate sensitivity: If take rate compresses from 0.63% to 0.45% (regulator action on interchange), SOM drops to $2.0B. Plan still 8% of revised SOM.
Capture sensitivity: If we capture only 3% (vs 5.1% modeled), revenue is $341M — still meaningful, but pulls profitability from FY28 to FY29.
