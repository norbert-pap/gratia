Operating principles

1

Single accountable exec per stream

Named in roadmap, on board agenda quarterly, comp linked. No co-leads, no ambiguity.

2

Self-funding milestone in 14 months

Each stream produces measurable savings or revenue offset by month 14. No more 5-year transformation timelines.

3

Quarterly kill-or-double review

Each stream reviewed at the quarterly board. We commit explicitly to either kill or double-down. No drift.

What we're not doing

No core system replacement. Policy admin platform stays. Total cost / risk doesn't pencil. We can wrap APIs around it.
No M&A. 4 acquisition candidates evaluated; all required integration capacity we don't have. Revisit FY28.
No new geographic expansion. Cost-of-capital for state regulatory entry too high vs. opportunity. Hold at current 22 states.
