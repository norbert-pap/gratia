What we did

Value-stream mapping (3 plants). Identified 9 high-impact bottlenecks; prioritized 4 lines for kaizen.
12 kaizen events. 5-day rapid improvement workshops; cross-functional teams of 8 each.
Standardized work. Documented best-known method for 38 critical operations.
Daily management. Tier-1 huddles, andon system, weekly leader walks.

Sustaining mechanisms

Internal kaizen team (4 FTE). Trained and certified during engagement; runs 1 event per month.
Idea-system platform. Operators submit improvement ideas; 70% implementation target.
Quarterly leader gemba. Plant manager + ops VP visit each cell quarterly.
Shop-floor scorecards. Visual daily metrics at every cell — refreshed at shift start.
