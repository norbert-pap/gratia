Analyst levels & promotion

Seven levels (A1, A2, A3, A4, L1, M1) plus a separate engineer track (E1). Promotion is a function of three things: AQS, specialization tier scores, and review outcomes on real deliverables.

SECTION 03
Analyst levels and what we expect
Seven levels: A1, A2, A3, A4, L1, M1, plus a separate engineer track (E1).
A1AQS 70+
Apprentice analyst
Execute well-defined tasks with supervision. Learn Gratia quality standards and workflows.
Spec: no minimum · Supervision: required on all client deliverables.
Typical work: budget templates, competitor profiles, basic charts, process docs, task lists.
A2AQS 80+
Associate business analyst
Work independently on standard deliverables. Deliver quality work consistently.
Spec: 1+ at Familiar (SS 50+) · Supervision: light touch, spot checks.
Typical work: 3-statement models, investor pitch decks, TAM/SAM, Tableau dashboards, RFP responses.
A3AQS 90+
Business analyst
Handle complex work with direct client interface. Lead workstreams and deliver reference-quality work.
Spec: 1+ Expert (85+), 2+ Proficient (70+) · Supervision: none required.
Typical work: DCF valuations, Series B+ pitch decks, market entry strategy, M&A analysis, board presentations.
A4AQS 95+
Senior business analyst
Recognized domain expert. Handle the most challenging problems. Create standards for the team.
Spec: 2+ Expert, 3+ Proficient · Supervision: provides oversight to others.
Typical work: corporate strategy, M&A DD, IPO roadshows, enterprise transformation, turnaround.
L1AQS 95+ sustained
Lead business analyst
Lead multi-analyst engagements. Accountable for project-level delivery while still contributing hands-on.
Spec: 3+ Expert, 4+ Proficient · Supervision: reviews team deliverables.
Typical work: workstream planning, QA review, client status meetings, analyst coaching.
M1AQS 95+ on team
Business analyst manager
Manage analyst teams at scale. Own talent development, quality standards, and operational excellence.
Spec: Expert in 3+, deep in all 9 · Supervision: sets and enforces standards.
Typical work: capacity planning, performance reviews, QA program oversight, client relationships.
E1N/A · engineer track
AI / automation engineer
Build and maintain client automations using no-code / low-code platforms, lightweight scripting, and AI tooling.
Track: separate from A1–A4 · Supervision: self-directed on engagements.
Typical work: Zapier / Make / n8n, Kissflow, Airtable, AI assistants, document processing.
SECTION 04
Promotion criteria
Promotion is a function of three things: AQS, specialization tier scores, and review outcomes on real deliverables.
A1→A2
  • AQS consistently 80+
  • 1+ specialization at Familiar (SS 50+)
  • 3+ deliverables Passed/Exceeded
  • Less supervision needed
A2→A3
  • AQS consistently 90+
  • 1+ Expert (SS 85+)
  • 2+ Proficient or above
  • 5+ deliverables Exceeded
  • 4.5+ avg client rating
A3→A4
  • AQS consistently 95+
  • 2+ Expert (SS 85+)
  • 3+ Proficient or above
  • Reference-quality work
  • Contributes to team standards
A4→L1
  • AQS 95+ sustained
  • 3+ Expert (SS 85+)
  • 4+ Proficient (SS 70+)
  • Demonstrated mentoring impact
L1→M1
  • Manages 5+ analysts
  • Track record developing analysts (A1→A2, A2→A3)
  • VP+ client relationships
  • Operational maturity
Soft restriction on decline. If an analyst's AQS drops below their level threshold for a sustained period, they are not demoted immediately. Instead they keep their title, are flagged internally (not visible to clients), and are not matched to work at their level until AQS recovers. This gives time to recover without the demoralizing impact of a formal demotion.
