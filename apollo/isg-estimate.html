01 · Project brief

Scope, objectives, timing

Engagement summary

Gratia is enabling and supporting the Apollo Kissflow path to production, a six-person team carrying the Sprint 7–9 build through to a May 30 go-live and into a 4–8 week Hypercare. The team covers project leadership, change and delivery, analyst capacity, developer build, and AI-assisted prototyping and QA.

The Sprint 7–9 plan brings all remaining team workflows forward and locks the final production version. Engineering QA and UAT close in week of May 11, scope freezes the following week, and a full simulation runs into the production go/no-go on May 28.

Hypercare begins on June 1, with leadership review at the end of the first week. Open items: confirm Gino's go-forward weekly load, lock Kirsty's L1 cadence, and align on the spend-to-date cut-off.
