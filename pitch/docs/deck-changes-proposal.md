# Deck Changes: "Marketplace Proof → FoW Vision" Arc

## Philosophy
Lead with what you've built and proven. Let the traction earn the right to talk about vision. FoW framing enters gradually — fully landing on the vision slide, not the cover.

---

## SLIDE 4 — COVER

**Current:**
> "The workforce infrastructure that creates its own supply."
> AI-trained analysts, matched to decision-grade work — in days, not weeks.

**Proposed:**
> "Decision-grade analysts. 48 hours. No hiring, no consulting."
> AI-vetted, AI-coached, and matched to the work — so you get the answer, not just a draft.

**Why:** "Workforce infrastructure" is the punchline, not the opener. The new cover says what you *do* in concrete terms. An investor reads this and thinks "oh, this is a marketplace that actually works." The FoW reveal comes later.

---

## SLIDE 5 — PROBLEM / DEMAND

**No structural changes.** The demand slide already works — spiky work, slow consulting, AI can't own the answer. This is universally true regardless of framing.

**One small text tweak in the neg-summary:**

**Current closer:**
> "Companies need skilled people who can own outcomes — fast, and at a reasonable price."

**Proposed closer:**
> "Every option is broken. Companies need skilled people who can own outcomes — fast, and at a price that makes sense."

**Why:** Slightly stronger setup. "Every option is broken" earns the solution slide.

---

## SLIDE 6 — PROBLEM / SUPPLY

**Current structure:** 3 points
1. Millions of skilled people. No way in.
2. Great talent, buried in unvetted noise (Upwork)
3. AI brings apprenticeship back ← **this is the problem**

**Proposed structure:** 3 points — reorder + soften point 3
1. Millions of skilled people. No way in. *(unchanged)*
2. Great talent, buried in unvetted noise *(unchanged)*
3. **NEW: "The training pipeline is gone — and nothing replaced it"**
   > Companies used to train junior analysts into senior ones. That took years and a full-time seat. Now headcount is flat, AI handles the drafts, and nobody invests in building people anymore. The training pipeline that created skilled analysts is eroding — and freelance platforms never had one.

**Current closer:**
> "Nobody built the apprenticeship system for the AI age. We did."

**Proposed closer:**
> "The talent exists. The system to vet them, develop them, and make them trustworthy doesn't. That's what we built."

**Why:** Point 3 was making a FoW thesis claim ("AI brings apprenticeship back") before you'd earned the right. The new version states the *problem* — training pipelines are disappearing — without claiming you've solved it with AI coaching (which needs more data). The closer now sets up the solution slide without overclaiming.

---

## SLIDE 7 — SOLUTION

**Current title:**
> "We don't *just* find great analysts. We create them — then match them to the work."

**Proposed title:**
> "We vet, develop, and match analysts — so the work gets done right the first time."

**Current subtitle:**
> "You're buying people + AI as a package. Skilled analysts backed by a personal AI coach, automated scoping, and transparent delivery. Consulting quality at a price that makes sense."

**Proposed subtitle:**
> "Rigorous vetting, AI-assisted coaching during delivery, automated scoping, and full transparency. Consulting quality, marketplace speed, at a price that makes sense."

**Why:** "We create them" is a strong claim that the AI coaching data doesn't fully support yet. "We vet, develop, and match" is provably true today. "AI-assisted coaching" is honest — it's there, it works, but it's an assist, not the whole story. This lets you talk about GraceAI in the meeting without the slide overclaiming.

---

## SLIDE 8 — WHY NOW

**Current point 2 title:** "AI brings apprenticeship back"

**Proposed point 2 title:** "AI makes real-time coaching possible — for the first time"

**Current point 2 text:** references "personal AI coach" and "what used to take years of apprenticeship, we compress into weeks"

**Proposed point 2 text:**
> "For the first time, an AI can sit alongside an analyst during a live project — flagging errors, suggesting methodology, coaching in real time. This wasn't possible two years ago. It changes the economics of training completely."

**Why:** Same idea, but framed as a technology unlock ("this is now possible") rather than a solved problem ("we compress years into weeks"). More believable at your current data stage.

## SLIDE 20 — VISION ← This is where FoW fully lands

**Current title:** "Analytical services are the wedge. The platform is the prize."

**Proposed title:** "We proved the marketplace works. Here's why it becomes infrastructure."

**Current subtitle:**
> "Every category we enter follows the same playbook: AI upskills the supply, the platform matches it, and the data compounds."

**Proposed subtitle:**
> "The marketplace traction proves the model. The AI coaching layer is what makes it defensible and scalable. Every category follows the same playbook."

**Why:** This is the FoW reveal moment. You've spent 10+ slides proving the marketplace works. The conditional "we earn this when the data proves it" stays — it's honest and investors respect it.
