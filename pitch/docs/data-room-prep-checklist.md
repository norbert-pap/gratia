# Gratia Data Room — Investor Prep Checklist

*What you need ready before meetings. Organized by what VCs will ask first.*

---

## Priority 1: They Will Ask In The First Meeting

### Financials & Unit Economics
- [ ] **Monthly GMV** — Total value of analyst hours billed through Gratia. Benchmark: $100K-$150K/mo at your stage.
- [ ] **Monthly net revenue** — Your take-rate revenue after analyst payouts. Benchmark: $30K-$50K/mo.
- [ ] **Actual take rate** — Currently shown as [X]% in deck. Benchmark: 30-40% for managed talent marketplaces (Toptal ~30%, staffing 30-50%).
- [ ] **Actual gross margin** — Currently shown as [Y]% in deck. Benchmark: 55-65% on net revenue. Show current AND projected at scale.
- [ ] **MoM revenue growth rate** — Benchmark: 15-20% MoM at this stage. You show quarterly in the deck; have monthly ready.
- [ ] **Revenue breakdown by segment** — What % is SMB vs. enterprise? What % is Apollo vs. rest? Customer concentration risk is the #1 question.
- [ ] **Burn rate and runway** — At current spend, how many months? Post-raise, how many months? Path to breakeven at what revenue?
- [ ] **Average engagement value by segment** — "$4.5K avg SMB" is in the deck. Have enterprise avg ready too (the Apollo number changes the blended average).

### Traction Proof
- [ ] **Revenue chart (monthly, not just quarterly)** — Shows trajectory more precisely. VCs want to see month-over-month, not smoothed quarters.
- [ ] **Cohort retention data** — 85% repeat rate is strong, but break it down: How many projects does the avg client do? What's 3-month, 6-month, 12-month retention?
- [ ] **LTV:CAC ratio** — Lifetime value of a client vs. cost to acquire. Benchmark: 3:1+ minimum. If you don't have formal CAC, estimate it.
- [ ] **CAC payback period** — Months to recoup acquisition cost. Benchmark: <6 months ideal.
- [ ] **Client list (anonymized or named)** — Beyond Apollo, who are your top 10 clients? Industry, deal size, repeat status.
- [ ] **Win rate on enterprise sales** — How many enterprise pilots before Apollo? How many didn't convert? FJ Labs will ask this directly.

### Product
- [ ] **Product screenshots (real, not placeholder)** — All three VC personas flagged this. Slides 11-14 are all "UI screenshot" placeholders. This must be fixed before any meeting.
- [ ] **Demo video (90 seconds)** — For async deck sends. Show the scoping → matching → coaching → credentialing flow.
- [ ] **GraceAI evidence** — Before/after quality metrics with AI coaching vs. without. a16z will drill into this.

## Priority 2: They Will Ask In Follow-up / Due Diligence

### Flywheel Compounding Evidence
*Every VC persona asked for this. It's the single biggest gap.*

- [ ] **Month 1 vs. Month 12 comparison** — Pick 2-3 metrics that prove the system got better: match accuracy, time-to-match, analyst quality scores, revision rates, scoping speed.
- [ ] **AI automation rate over time** — What % of workflow is now AI-handled vs. manual? The "2-day → 2-hour" claim needs a number.
- [ ] **Project success rate trend** — Has first-attempt quality improved? Fewer revision cycles per project over time?
- [ ] **Matching speed trend** — Time from brief to matched analyst, Month 1 vs. now.

### Supply-Side Health
- [ ] **Analyst utilization rate** — % of vetted analysts actively billing in any given month. Shows supply-side health.
- [ ] **Analyst retention rate** — % of analysts who stay on platform >6 months. Two-sided marketplace health signal.
- [ ] **Revenue per analyst** — Annual revenue generated per active analyst. Shows scalability of supply.
- [ ] **New supply sourcing** — Of 150+ analysts, how many are new vs. returning? FJ Labs wants to know if you're "creating supply" or "optimizing allocation."
- [ ] **Projects per analyst distribution** — 270 projects / 150 analysts = 1.8 avg. But is it 20 analysts doing 10 projects each, or 150 doing 1-2? Show the distribution.
- [ ] **Analyst poaching protection** — What stops a client from hiring an analyst directly? Non-compete? Value prop? Platform lock-in? GC will ask.
- [ ] **Tier distribution** — How many analysts at each pricing tier? Where does revenue concentrate?

### Enterprise Motion
- [ ] **Enterprise sales playbook (documented)** — Apollo timeline: first contact → pilot → close. How long? What was the motion?
- [ ] **Enterprise pipeline** — Beyond Apollo, how many enterprise prospects are in pipeline? What stage?
- [ ] **Enterprise vs. SMB unit economics** — Different LTV, CAC, engagement size, sales cycle. Break them out.
- [ ] **Expansion revenue %** — % of revenue from existing clients expanding scope. The Austin/Endeavor testimonial hints at this — quantify it.
- [ ] **Apollo reference readiness** — Is Apollo willing to speak with investors? "Referenceable customer" is in the deck; confirm it's real.

### Competitive Defensibility
- [ ] **Competitive scenario plan** — "What if Upwork launches 'Upwork Pro'?" or "What if McKinsey builds in-house AI training?" Have 1-page responses ready for each.
- [ ] **Data moat quantification** — 2,000+ projects = how much training data? How many scoping templates, credentialing signals, matching patterns? Make it concrete.
- [ ] **"Why can't this be copied?" one-pager** — The answer VCs want: "System advantage + data compound + time head start. Copying one layer doesn't help; you need all five + the data from 2,000+ projects."

## Priority 3: Nice to Have / Differentiating

### Metrics That Separate Good Decks From Great Ones

- [ ] **NPS split by side** — You show 88.2 blended. If both sides are 80+, show them separately for extra impact.
- [ ] **"Decision-grade" definition** — a16z will ask: What makes work "decision-grade"? Define it operationally. Quality score? Revision rate? Client sign-off rate?
- [ ] **Analyst career progression data** — How many analysts have moved up tiers? How many started as Apprentice and are now Analyst or Senior? This proves "we create analysts."
- [ ] **Revenue per dollar of capital raised** — You raised $1.2M and generated $2M revenue. That's $1.67 revenue per dollar invested. If you bootstrapped most of this, say so prominently.
- [ ] **Adjacent vertical research** — If you claim "the playbook transfers," have preliminary research on which category is next and why. Legal? Strategy? Technical research?

6. **Supply-side analysis** — Analyst retention, utilization, tier progression

9. **Customer references** — 3-5 clients willing to take a call (Apollo + others)
10. **Analyst references** — 2-3 analysts willing to speak about career impact
11. **Technical architecture overview** — For AI-focused investors (a16z type). GraceAI stack, data pipeline, model approach.
